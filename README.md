## About

I work in computational mechanics, developing numerical formulations and research software for structural and material modelling. My work connects continuum mechanics, constitutive modelling, and numerical implementation, with a particular focus on nonlinear structures and coupled thermomechanical problems.

## Research & Development

- **Structural mechanics** — beam, plate, and shell formulations, including geometrically exact models.
- **Numerical methods** — finite element and isogeometric analysis, with an emphasis on collocation methods.
- **Constitutive modelling** — smart materials, shape-memory behaviour, and thermo-viscoelasticity.
- **Solver development** — implementation, verification, and assessment of numerical methods.
- **Applications** — shape-changing structures, 4D printing, and patient-specific stents.
