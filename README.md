## Hi there, welcome to my profile! 👋

I'm a researcher in Computational Mechanics specializing in advanced simulation and numerical methods, including Finite Element Analysis (FEA) and Isogeometric Analysis (IGA).

🏫 **Affiliation**:  
Currently, I am affiliated with the University of Florence (Italy) and Universitat Politècnica de Catalunya (UPC), Spain, through the International Centre for Numerical Methods in Engineering (CIMNE), contributing to cutting-edge research in structural mechanics.

🔬 **Research Focus**:  
My work centers on:  
- Modeling and optimization of beam, plate, and shell structures  
- Numerical methods (FEA, IGA, etc.) for solid and structural mechanics  
- Material modeling (Composite, FGMs, etc.)  

- Optimization techniques  

💻 **Software & Projects**:  
I am actively co-developing and contributing to the following:  
- **[farshadrhni](https://github.com/farshadrhni)**: My GitHub repository hosting various computational mechanics projects.  
- Research codes for isogeometric analysis and finite element simulations of FGMs and shell structures.
